# Privacy Policy — CaptionsAI

**Last updated: 15 September 2026**

CaptionsAI is made by an independent developer. This policy describes what the
app does with your information. It is short because the app does very little
with it.

## The short version

CaptionsAI does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information.
There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
third-party SDK. The developer has no server, and the app has no way to send
your videos anywhere.

## Your videos and captions

Videos you import, the captions generated from them, the styles you choose and
the files you export all stay in the app's private storage on your device. They
are never uploaded. Deleting a project removes its video file from your device;
deleting the app removes everything.

When you export a video, it is saved to your own photo library, with your
permission. That copy is yours and is not shared with anyone.

## Speech recognition — please read this part

Captions are generated using Apple's Speech framework.

Wherever your device and language support **on-device** speech recognition,
CaptionsAI requires it, and your audio never leaves your device.

Where on-device recognition is **not** available — which depends on your device
model and the language you are transcribing — Apple's Speech framework
processes the audio on **Apple's servers** instead. In that case a portion of
your video's audio is sent to Apple and handled under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). CaptionsAI does
not receive, see or store that data, and it is never sent to the developer or
to anyone else.

The app does not currently tell you which of these two paths it used. If
guaranteed on-device processing matters to you, please contact the developer
before relying on the app for sensitive material.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Photo library** — to let you choose a video to caption, and to save the
  video you export. The app reads only the video you pick.
- **Speech recognition** and **microphone** — required by Apple's Speech
  framework to turn your video's audio into captions. The app does not record
  from your microphone.

## Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment
details. The app asks Apple whether a subscription is currently active and
nothing more.

## Feedback you choose to send

If you use the in-app feedback or feature-request button, the app opens a
pre-filled email for you to review and edit before you send it. That message
contains only what you type plus the app version, the iOS version and your
region — all of it visible to you in the message body, and all of it deletable
before sending. Nothing about your videos, captions, file names or projects is
included. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

If you email the developer, the developer has your email address and whatever
you wrote, for as long as the message sits in the mailbox. It is not added to
any list.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
